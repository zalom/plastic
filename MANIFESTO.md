# Why I built Plastic

I kept losing the thread of my own work.

I would leave a project for a day and come back to a blank. The code was
still there, but the reasoning behind it was gone. Why did I choose this?
What was I about to do next? A session would end, and the next one started
from nothing. The agent forgot, and so did I.

Plastic is my answer to that. Here is what I believe.

## Memory is the result of work, not a feature

You should not have to write documentation about your work after you finish
it. You should do the work in a way that leaves a record on its own.

In Plastic you do the work as an intent: a short file that says what you
want, why, how you plan to get there, and what happened. When you finish,
the record is already written. Ask where an idea came from, or why a choice
was made, and the answer is your own history.

## Two founding systems

Plastic stands on two ideas.

The first is a System for the Brain. It is the Zettelkasten method: small,
linked notes that grow into more than their sum. Each intent links to the
intents that shaped it and the ones it led to. The name borrows from
neuroplasticity, the way a brain rewires itself as it learns.

The second is a System for the Work. It separates the fixed part of work
from the creative part.

## The blueprint is fixed, the thinking stays free

The blueprint is the shape: the conventions, the templates, the layout, and
the four stages every intent moves through. That shape is fixed, so any
person or agent meets the same rails and can pick up where another left off.

The thinking is what to build and how to build it. That stays free. A human
or an agent does the reasoning, and Plastic never replaces that judgment. It
only steers and checks it.

Agents feel the rails. You feel a conversation.

## Plain files you own

Your work lives in plain Markdown files under `~/.plastic/`, tracked in git.
There is no database you cannot open, and no service that holds your memory
somewhere else. You can read every intent with your own eyes, and you own
all of it.

## Honest about beta

Plastic is in beta. It works, and I use it every day, but it is still
moving. I would rather tell you that than pretend otherwise. Install it, and
let the record grow with you.
