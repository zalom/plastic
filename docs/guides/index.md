# Guides

Task-oriented walkthroughs for people meeting Plastic for the first time,
through choosing a way of working, to understanding the bigger picture.

1. [Your first intent in 10 minutes](your-first-intent-in-10-minutes.md).
   For a brand-new user: install Plastic and deliver one small intent, start
   to finish.
2. [Pick your mode](pick-your-mode.md). For someone deciding how much control
   to keep: manual, guided, or auto, explained honestly.
3. [What the gates are telling you](what-the-gates-are-telling-you.md). For
   someone who just hit a deny message and wants to know the fix.
4. [Reading a delivered intent](reading-a-delivered-intent.md). For someone
   opening a finished intent and wanting to know fast what actually shipped.
5. [Working without QMD and Serena](working-without-qmd-and-serena.md). For
   someone who has not installed these optional tools and wants to know what
   changes.
6. [Using Plastic with Claude Code](using-plastic-with-claude-code.md). For
   someone ready to use Plastic day to day: the guided/auto ladder and
   roadmap-driven delivery.
